## Council-Identified Constraints

### S-4. The next target's revalidation and the stage's listings
**Raised by:** systems-architect (S-4, LOW)
**Concern:** The draft's D2 made the stage's post-check listing serve as the
next target's revalidation ref listing, so that a merge target moved during a
branch stage would be caught there. No requirement said so, and it collided
with feature 004's T085 ("after a plain `removed`, its rescan's three
repository children serve as the next target's repository part, counted in
that target's `probes_performed`"; feature 004 `tasks.md` at `4e3af0a`, lines
1455-1463) and with this change's own counting sentence, so a target's
`probes_performed` had two readings and D2's stated purpose had no test.
**Design impact:** D2's substitution is deleted. The next target's
revalidation keeps change 1's sourcing, the rescan's three repository children
as T085 says, and T085 and the counting are untouched. The stage's re-read and
post-check are counted in the stage's own `probes_performed`, in
`branch_retirement`, and never reused. A merge target moved by a concurrent
writer during a branch stage is caught by the next target's own re-read before
its branch child, which compares the merge target under ruling A-14, and that
branch is retained with `state-changed`; concurrent writers stay outside the
contract (`design.md` D2, D4 and D5).
